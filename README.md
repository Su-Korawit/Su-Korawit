</div>

Thai students have to choose a career, a major and a university at an age when most of us can't say what we want for lunch. The advice usually comes as a pamphlet or a rankings list. Neither helps with the part that matters, which is doing something about it every day.

Roller Coaster treats that as a game.

| 🧬 Start | 🎯 Plan | ⚔️ Play | 🤝 Connect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick your MBTI. A pixel-art character for your type becomes your avatar. | Choose a career, a skill track, a university and a major. | Daily tasks earn coins and keep a streak alive. Spend coins on skills like Ignite, a 5-second countdown, or Capsule, a lock timer with a bonus. | Real-time chat, a school map and a match score put you next to students on the same road. |

Most of the psychology lives in the small mechanics. Streaks and rewards pull you back. A Gemini-powered coach writes a small daily task from the goal you pinned, then reflects on how it went. I spent a lot of time on those prompts.

The code stays in a private repo, so the live app is the thing to look at. There's also a demo extension on top of it, which goes from a chat with a shop assistant to a PromptPay QR code and a slip upload.
