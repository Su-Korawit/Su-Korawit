## Main project: Roller Coaster

**[Try it live](https://design-rollercoaster.web.app/)** · now called *I Love TCAS*

Thai students are asked to pick a career, a major and a university at about the age when most of us can't say what we want for lunch. The advice usually comes as a pamphlet or a rankings list. Neither helps much with the part that actually matters, which is doing something about it every day.

Roller Coaster is a mobile-first web app that treats that as a game. You start by picking your MBTI type, and a pixel-art character for that type becomes your avatar. From there you choose a career, a skill track, a university and a major. The app turns the plan into daily tasks. Finish them and you earn coins and keep a streak going. Spend the coins on skills that change how you work, like a five-second countdown before a task starts, or a lock timer that pays a bonus if you stay on it.

Most of the psychology is in the small mechanics. Streaks and rewards push you to show up. An AI coach (Gemini) writes a small daily task from the goal you pinned and reflects on how it went when you finish. Real-time chat, a school map and a match score put you in touch with other students working on the same exams.

It runs on React 19, Vite, Tailwind, Zustand and Firebase, with Phaser 3 for the animated home screen and Leaflet for the school map. The code lives in a private repo, so the live app is the thing to look at. There's also a payment-and-AI-chat extension built on top of it, a demo that goes from a chat with a shop assistant to a PromptPay QR code and a slip upload.

## Other things I've built

**[singjourney](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/singjourney)** is an AI travel planner for Sing Buri, my entry for the I-New Gen Award 2027. Planning a trip is mostly a decision problem, and people give up when there are too many options, so its job is to cut them down.

**[mytcas-api](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/mytcas-api)** wraps the data on mytcas.com so universities, faculties, programmes and application rounds come back as clean data. It ships with React hooks and a Vercel serverless setup.

**[ApexDroneKit](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/ApexDroneKit)** is a Python library for flying the APEX GD-149 drone from code. A few lines that make a drone take off teach more than a chapter on loops.

**[TalkAlways](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/TalkAlways)** is the bilingual blog and writing space I'm working on now. Writing something down is the cheapest way I know to find out what I think.

I also write up what I learn, including the parts I got wrong first, at [ikkyusanstudy.me](https://ikkyusanstudy.me).
