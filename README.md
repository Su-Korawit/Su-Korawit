# Korawit Suporn

Student at SBR School, writing code on the side of everything else. Most people know me as Ikkyusan.

I got into computers because I like making a machine do exactly what I told it to. I got interested in psychology because people almost never do. Nearly everything I build sits somewhere between those two facts.

## What I've been building

I keep running into the same question: why does a tool that works perfectly still go unused?

**[singjourney](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/singjourney)** started there. It's an AI travel planner for Sing Buri, my home province, and it's my entry for the I-New Gen Award 2027. Planning a trip is mostly a decision problem, and people give up on it when there are too many choices, so the planner's job is to cut the choices down.

**[mytcas-api](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/mytcas-api)** came from my own year of staring at TCAS pages. The data on mytcas.com is all public, but you can't build anything on top of it. I wrapped it so universities, faculties, programmes and application rounds come back as clean data, with React hooks and a Vercel serverless setup for anyone who wants to use it.

**[ApexDroneKit](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/ApexDroneKit)** is a Python library for flying the APEX GD-149 drone from code. A drone is more fun to learn on than a terminal, and a few lines that make it take off teach more than a chapter on loops.

**[TalkAlways](https://github.com/Su-Korawit/TalkAlways)** is what I'm working on now: a bilingual blog and writing space. Writing a thing down is the cheapest way I know to find out what I actually think, and I wanted a place that makes that easy in both Thai and English.

I also keep [a blog](https://ikkyusanstudy.me) where I write up what I learn, including the parts I got wrong first.
